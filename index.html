A story in one breath

In every lifetime,
I’d find you.

We met on a rain-softened Tuesday, two strangers reaching for the same dog-eared book, and somewhere between your shy apology and my nervous laugh the whole world seemed to lean closer; since then, love has lived in the smallest places—in coffee left warm beside the bed, in long roads with no destination, in your hand finding mine beneath crowded tables—and through every bright morning and difficult season, I have chosen you, only to discover that choosing you has always felt less like a decision and more like remembering the way home.

Always yours,Elena & Theo
